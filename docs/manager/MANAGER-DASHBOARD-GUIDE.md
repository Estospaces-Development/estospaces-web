Estospaces gives managers one operating system for live response, property publishing, shortlist sharing, fast-track progression, and client coordination. This guide is written from the manager point of view. It explains what each part of the manager dashboard is for, how to stay operationally ready, when the Estospaces Team reviews your profile or properties, and what to do when a case needs escalation instead of another guess.

Use this guide as your working manual. It is designed to answer the questions that matter in live operations: who owns the next action, what stage the lead is in, where to manage it, when to respond, why a review may be pending, how to keep a case moving, which properties can be shared, how long a live request stays actionable, and what happens when documents, approvals, or compliance checks intervene.

> Managers succeed fastest on Estospaces when they treat speed and relevance as partners. Accept only when you can follow through, shortlist only what genuinely fits, and keep every active case tied to one clear next step.

## Property Inventory Lifecycle

### Who this is for
Managers responsible for listing quality and availability.

### What it is
Your inventory lifecycle is the full path from draft creation to live publication and later inactive outcomes such as sold, rented, or rejected.

### Where to do it
- Property list: [/manager/dashboard/properties](/manager/dashboard/properties)
- Add property: [/manager/dashboard/properties/add](/manager/dashboard/properties/add)

### Which lifecycle states matter most

| Status | What it means | Can it usually be shared? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Still being prepared | No |
| Pending approval | Submitted and awaiting Estospaces Team review | Usually no |
| Published / Active / Online | Live and available on the platform | Yes |
| Sold / Rented | No longer available | No |
| Rejected | Not approved in its current form | No |

### Why this matters
Managers often lose time by assuming a newly created property is instantly shareable. In practice, publication readiness and review status determine whether a listing can enter a live user journey.

### How to keep inventory operational
- Keep titles, media, pricing, and status accurate.
- Do not rely on memory; confirm the actual listing status in the platform.
- Remove or update outdated listings promptly.
- Re-check availability before sharing any shortlist.

### What the Estospaces Team reviews for properties
The Estospaces Team may review property completeness, policy compliance, verification alignment, and publication readiness before the listing becomes fully live.

## Creating, Editing, and Submitting Properties

### Who this is for
Managers responsible for adding and maintaining supply on the platform.

### What it is
This is the listing preparation workflow that turns raw property information into a trustworthy, publishable Estospaces listing.

### Where to do it
- Add property: [/manager/dashboard/properties/add](/manager/dashboard/properties/add)
- Edit property: [/manager/dashboard/properties](/manager/dashboard/properties)

### When to use it
Use it when bringing on new inventory, correcting outdated information, or resubmitting a listing that needs revision.

### Why it matters
Quality at this stage shapes everything that comes later. Weak listings lead to weak shortlists, confused users, avoidable questions, and slower approvals.

### How to create a property well
1. Gather complete address, pricing, and property-type details first.
2. Upload clear, relevant visuals and accurate descriptive information.
3. Review every field before submission.
4. Submit only when the listing is genuinely review-ready.

### Which questions should you answer before submitting
- Is the property genuinely available?
- Is the pricing current?
- Is the description accurate rather than aspirational?
- Would I feel comfortable sharing this listing with a client today if it were approved?

### How often should inventory be reviewed
Review active inventory regularly, especially after market changes, manager-side edits, or property availability changes.

### What happens after submission
The listing typically enters a review state such as pending approval, then moves toward publication or back to revision if issues are identified.

### What if a property remains pending too long
Confirm that the submission is complete, check notifications, and then use support if the listing appears stuck without explanation.

## The 10-Minute Live Response Workflow

### Who this is for
Managers handling incoming user broker requests.

### What it is
This is the time-sensitive intake workflow where a user asks for broker help and available managers can accept the opportunity.

### Where to do it
- Leads and clients: [/manager/leads](/manager/leads)
- Dashboard: [/manager/dashboard](/manager/dashboard)

### When to use it
Use it whenever you are live and available to respond to new user requests.

### Why it matters
The live response stage defines the quality of the handoff. Accepting too slowly, too late, or without real capacity can create a poor experience for both the user and your own workload.

### How to handle the live window
1. Go live only when you are ready to respond quickly.
2. Review the brief immediately.
3. Accept only if you can genuinely progress the case afterward.
4. Once accepted, move quickly into the matched workspace.

### Which requests should you accept
Accept requests that:
- fit your operating area
- fit your available inventory
- fit your current capacity
- are realistic for active follow-through

### How long is the live stage
The live broker response stage is built around a 10-minute decision window. That is short by design, so your readiness before the request arrives matters.

### What happens if you do not respond in time
The lead may expire, re-route, or no longer behave like an active request. Treat inactive requests as closed intake, not live opportunities.

### What if you accepted but the lead says it is no longer active
Move to the live matched workspace or the next active journey surface instead of trying to repeat stale lead-stage actions. If the state is inconsistent, escalate with support.

## Matched Workspaces and Portfolio Sharing

### Who this is for
Managers who have already accepted a user request.

### What it is
The matched workspace is where you turn a live acceptance into a valuable client handoff by sharing relevant property options.

### Where to do it
- Leads and clients: [/manager/leads](/manager/leads)
- Property inventory: [/manager/dashboard/properties](/manager/dashboard/properties)

### Why it matters
This is where users judge your usefulness. A strong shortlist creates confidence. A weak, generic, or unavailable shortlist slows the journey immediately.

### How to shortlist properly
1. Re-read the user brief before selecting properties.
2. Confirm each property is live and genuinely available.
3. Rank the options in the order you would personally present them.
4. Share only the properties you can defend with confidence.

### Which properties should not be shared
- draft listings
- pending approval listings unless the platform explicitly allows it
- sold or rented listings
- mismatched or speculative options
- properties you know are operationally unavailable

### How many should you share
There is no single magic number, but the shortlist should feel curated rather than overloaded. Relevance matters more than volume.

### What happens after sharing
The user receives the shared portfolio in their workspace, reviews the options, and may select one property to activate the next phase.

### What if the user does not select a property
Use messages to clarify fit, revisit criteria, and improve the shortlist rather than assuming silence means disinterest.

Managers who want to understand where platform-side approval or review affects their work.

The Estospaces Team handles the review layers that protect quality, compliance, trust, and platform integrity.

### Where this review is usually felt
- manager verification
- property publication
- user or document verification touchpoints
- fast-track readiness checkpoints
- compliance-sensitive contract or payment transitions

### Why it matters
Understanding review ownership helps you avoid blaming the wrong stage. Some delays are not user delays or manager delays. They are review dependencies.

### What the Estospaces Team may review

| Review area | Typical purpose | What helps it move faster |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manager profile and identity | Trust and compliance readiness | Accurate, complete manager data |
| Property submission | Publishing quality and policy compliance | Review-ready listings |

### Under what conditions review can slow you down

- inconsistent data

### What if you need a review decision urgently
Document the exact property, client, or case context and raise it through support. Clear escalation details usually resolve faster than vague urgency.

## Troubleshooting, Escalations, and "What If" Scenarios

### Who this is for
Managers dealing with real-world exceptions and unclear platform states.

### What this section covers
It answers the operational questions that appear when the ideal flow breaks down.

### What if my property does not appear in shortlist sharing
Check whether it is actually live and shareable. Draft, pending approval, rejected, sold, rented, or otherwise unavailable properties usually should not be shareable.

### What if a lead is no longer active
Do not keep acting on stale lead-stage actions. Move to the currently active matched workspace, fast-track case, or support path instead.

### What if a user says they cannot see my shortlist
Confirm the share action completed, confirm the correct client and request context, and verify that the properties shared were live and valid.

### What if the user selects a property I no longer want to recommend
Treat that as a high-priority clarification. Reconfirm availability and next steps immediately rather than letting the case drift.

### What if a property is still pending approval but the client is urgent
Explain the situation honestly and do not imply that an unapproved listing is guaranteed. Offer only realistic next options.

### What if documents are missing or poor quality
State exactly what is missing, why it matters, and what acceptable replacement will work. Ambiguous document requests cause repeat delays.

### What if I am unsure whether the blocker is mine or the platform's
Check this order:
1. property status
2. lead or case status
3. message trail
4. document readiness
5. notifications
6. support escalation

### So what is the best manager operating rule?
Keep every action anchored to the correct live state, the correct property status, and the correct next milestone. When any of those three are unclear, clarify first and act second.
