Estospaces helps you move from first search to active property completion with one connected experience. This guide is written from the user point of view. It explains what each part of the app is for, where to go next, what documents to gather, how long each stage usually takes, what the Estospaces Team may review, and what to do if a journey slows down.

Use this guide before you start, while you are active in a journey, and whenever you need to confirm whether the app is behaving the way it should. It is designed to answer the practical questions people usually ask: who should do this, what is this step, where do I do it, when does it matter, why does it matter, how does it work, which documents do I need, how much time should I allow, what does it cost, what happens next, and what if something goes wrong.

> Estospaces is most effective when you treat your dashboard as the single place to monitor progress. Search, messages, shortlist selection, fast-track, applications, contracts, and payments should all point back to the same active property journey.

## Account Setup, Sign-In, and Profile Readiness

### Who this is for
New users, returning users, and anyone preparing to move from casual browsing into a real transaction.

### What it is
Your profile is the identity and contact foundation for the rest of the platform. Messages, applications, verifications, and contracts depend on it being accurate.

- Notifications: [/user/dashboard/notifications](/user/dashboard/notifications)

### When to do it
Do this before your first broker request, before your first application, and before any contract or payment stage begins.

### Why it matters
If your name, contact details, or identity context are incomplete, later steps may pause while the Estospaces Team or a manager waits for corrections.

### How to complete profile readiness
1. Sign in and confirm you land on your dashboard.
2. Open your profile and confirm your name, email, phone number, and core details are correct.
3. Review settings and notification preferences so you do not miss messages or journey updates.
4. Watch for any prompts related to profile verification or missing information.

### Which details should be correct
- Your full legal name
- Active email address
- Active phone number
- Current residential information where relevant
- Any identity details that later documents must match

### How often should you review this
Review at least once before each major milestone:
- before requesting a broker
- before applying
- before signing
- before making a payment

### What the Estospaces Team may review here
The Estospaces Team may review identity consistency, completeness, and supporting information if your journey enters a stage that depends on verified details.

### What if something is wrong
If your details are outdated, fix them before you upload documents or sign anything. If the app does not let you update a required field, use [/user/dashboard/help](/user/dashboard/help).

## How to Buy a Home on Estospaces

### Who this is for
Users intending to purchase rather than rent.

### What it is
The buy journey combines discovery, broker support, property evaluation, fast-track progression, document readiness, negotiation context, contracts, and payment milestones.

### Where to do it
- Buy discovery: [/user/dashboard/discover?type=buy](/user/dashboard/discover?type=buy)
- Dashboard overview: [/user/dashboard](/user/dashboard)
- Messages: [/user/dashboard/messages](/user/dashboard/messages)
- Contracts: [/user/dashboard/contracts](/user/dashboard/contracts)
- Payments: [/user/dashboard/payments](/user/dashboard/payments)

### When to use this path
Use the buy path when you are evaluating a purchase and want long-term ownership rather than short-term occupancy.

### Why this path is different from rent
Buying typically requires deeper document preparation, more financial clarity, longer commitments, and stronger coordination around contracts and payment checkpoints.

### How the buy journey usually unfolds
1. Browse and save viable homes.
2. Decide whether to continue alone or request broker help.
3. Review any manager-shared shortlist.
4. Select a property when you are ready to commit to progression.
5. Move into the active fast-track or linked case journey.
6. Prepare identity, financial, and property-related supporting documents.
7. Review contracts and payment milestones carefully.

### Which documents matter most before buying
- Government-issued identity
- Proof of address
- Proof of income or financial standing
- Bank statements or finance evidence where required
- Any buyer-specific declarations requested in your journey

### How long can the buy journey take
The early selection stage can move quickly, especially once a property is chosen. The full completion timeline depends on document readiness, contract readiness, payment readiness, and review checkpoints.

### At what cost should you think ahead
Estospaces will show the platform-side property context and payment stages that apply inside your journey. Property price, legal expenses, financing charges, taxes, and third-party fees are external cost considerations you should budget for separately.

### What if you are not ready to buy immediately
You can continue exploring, save properties, and use broker guidance without forcing a full progression until your timing and documents are clearer.

## The 10-Minute Broker Request Flow

### Who this is for
Users who want active help from a manager or broker instead of browsing alone.

### What it is
The broker request is a live request that invites available managers to respond to your brief within a limited time window.

### Where to do it
You start from the user dashboard and then move into the live broker workspace on [/user/dashboard](/user/dashboard).

### When to use it
Use it when you want:
- a faster shortlist
- a more guided search
- help interpreting the market
- support when your needs are specific
- a quicker path toward action

### Why it matters
The 10-minute window creates urgency and clarity. It tells you whether a live manager is taking ownership soon or whether you should rematch or continue browsing another way.

### How it works
1. You create the request with your location, budget, and need.
2. The live workspace appears.
3. A countdown shows the response window.
4. A manager may accept and become your matched manager.
5. If accepted, the journey moves forward into property sharing.
6. If no one accepts, the app should guide you clearly toward the next option.

### Which details should you include
- Exact or approximate area
- Clear budget
- Buy or rent intent
- Timing expectations
- Any must-have requirements

### How long is the live window
The request window is designed around a 10-minute response flow. That does not mean your full journey ends in 10 minutes. It only means the live response stage is time-boxed.

### With whom are you interacting
You are inviting a relevant available manager or broker to step into the request and take the next action.

### What if nobody responds
You should see a clear expired or unresolved state rather than a silent failure. At that point you may retry, adjust your criteria, or continue browsing on your own.

## Shared Portfolio Review and Property Selection

### Who this is for
Users whose broker request has been accepted and who have received property options from a manager.

### What it is
This is the handoff stage where the manager shares a ranked set of properties and you decide which one becomes the active property for the journey.

### Where to do it
Return to your active broker workspace on [/user/dashboard](/user/dashboard) and open the shared property cards or the linked property detail page.

### When to use it
Use this stage after the manager is matched and the shortlist is available.

### Why it matters
Everything after this point becomes much easier if you choose one property deliberately. Fast-track, documents, applications, and contracts should all center around the selected property.

### How to review a shared portfolio
1. Read the manager's ranking and any notes.
2. Open each shared property detail page.
3. Compare price, address, suitability, and readiness.
4. Decide whether one property is strong enough to become the active property.
5. Choose only when you are comfortable moving forward with that specific home.

### Which questions should guide your choice
- Which property best fits your budget and timing?
- Which property still feels right after you view the full detail page?
- Which option do you feel confident progressing in messages, viewings, and paperwork?
- Which trade-offs are acceptable and which are not?

### What happens after you choose
The selected property should become the active property for the next phase. That means future fast-track, document, contract, and payment activity should stay tied to that property.

### What if none of the options fit
Do not force a selection. Re-open the discussion in messages, clarify the brief, or use support if the shortlist looks obviously mismatched.

Users who have chosen a property and entered the accelerated journey.

Fast-track is Estospaces' structured 24-hour guided progression after property selection. It is designed to keep momentum high and reduce avoidable waiting.

## Contracts, Payments, and Completion

### Who this is for
Users nearing formal commitment or completion.

### What it is
This is the stage where the property journey becomes legally and financially binding.

### Where to do it
- Contracts: [/user/dashboard/contracts](/user/dashboard/contracts)
- Payments: [/user/dashboard/payments](/user/dashboard/payments)
- Notifications: [/user/dashboard/notifications](/user/dashboard/notifications)

### When it matters
This stage matters once applications, selection, or approvals have reached the point where legal or financial next steps are active.

### Why it matters
This is the stage with the highest consequence. Slow reading, rushed assumptions, or missing evidence can be costly.

### How to handle contracts well
1. Confirm the contract relates to the correct property and journey.
2. Read every term carefully.
3. Make sure your identity and details match earlier documents.
4. Ask questions before signing, not after.
5. Keep message records if anything needs clarification.

### How to handle payments well
1. Confirm what the payment is for.
2. Confirm the amount and timing.
3. Confirm whether it is due now or after another milestone.
4. Check notifications and payment status after you act.

### How much should you expect to see in-app
Estospaces should surface the payment stages relevant to your live journey. Property price, rent, deposits, and any associated staged amounts belong to the journey context. External legal or financing costs are separate considerations.

### What if a contract or payment stage looks wrong
Stop and verify. Do not continue simply because the button is available. Use messages or support if the stage appears linked to the wrong property, wrong amount, or wrong status.

## Troubleshooting, Edge Cases, and "What If" Scenarios

### Who this is for
Anyone whose journey does not look straightforward.

### What this section answers
It covers the questions people usually ask when something feels delayed, unclear, or inconsistent.

### What if the broker request expires
The live request did not convert into a matched manager in time. Retry, adjust the brief, or continue with self-guided search rather than waiting in uncertainty.

### What if the manager shares no properties
Use messages first to clarify whether the shortlist is still being prepared or whether your brief needs to change.

### What if I selected the wrong property
Stop and confirm the current active state through your dashboard and messages immediately before uploading more documents or progressing to contract or payment stages.

### What if messages stop moving
Confirm the journey is still active, check notifications, then use support if you need intervention.

### What if applications or contracts show the wrong property
Treat that as a serious context issue. Do not continue blindly. Verify through messages or support before proceeding.

### What if my payment status does not update
Check the payments page, notifications, and any associated journey status. If the state remains unclear, use support with screenshots or exact timestamps.

### What if I need help deciding whether to buy or rent
Go back to the discovery phase, review your budget and timing honestly, and do not rush into fast-track until the active goal is clear.

### So what is the best operating habit?
The best habit is simple: start from the dashboard, confirm the active property and active stage, gather documents before the deadline, and use messages or support whenever a real-world decision depends on clarity.
